# Survey Instrument

HKU Student Housing Preferences Survey (anonymous, ~3 minutes to complete).


**Screening / demographics**
1. I am currently living in HKU dorm (halls/residential colleges)? — Yes / No
2. I am an — Local student / International student
3. Year of study — 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5-6
4. My faculty is — Arts / Business and Economics / Engineering / Science / Social Sciences / Law / Medicine / Education / Architecture
5. I am currently paying (or willing to pay) monthly housing fee of — Less than HK$2,000 / HK$2,000–3,999 / HK$4,000–5,999 / HK$6,000–7,999 / HK$8,000 or more
6. I am currently receiving — tuition-fee scholarship / full-ride (tuition + living expenses) / N/A

**Housing decision factors** (rate importance, 1 = not at all important, 5 = extremely important)
7. Price/Affordability
8. Commute time to campus
9. Privacy
10. Study environment and quiet hours
11. Social life and community building
12. Facilities and amenities (gym, laundry, common areas, etc.)
13. Rules and flexibility (guest policies, curfews, room decoration)

# Sustainability Extension 
**14. Sustainability features** (energy-efficient appliances/lighting, recycling and
waste-sorting facilities, water-saving fixtures)
`1 = Not at all important … 5 = Extremely important`

**15. Willingness to pay a premium for environmentally certified/green housing**
`1 = Not at all willing … 5 = Extremely willing`
